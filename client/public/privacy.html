Privacy Policy

Finance Tracker · Last updated 8 October 2026

Finance Tracker (financial.whoisakash.com) is a personal ledger for keeping track of savings certificates, deposits, insurance policies and similar investments. This page explains what the app does with your information.

What the app uses from your Google account

  • Your email address, to show which Google account your records are saved in.
  • Google Drive, limited to files the app creates (the drive.file permission). The app creates a "Finance Tracker" folder in your Drive and keeps your records, the documents you attach, a readable copy of your records (Google Sheet and Excel file) and backups there. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Where your information is kept

  • Your records and documents are stored in your own Google Drive, not on our servers.
  • The Google sign-in token that lets the app reach your Drive is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore so the connection keeps working. It is never shown to anyone.
  • On your device, the app keeps a copy of your records and any changes made while offline in the browser's storage, so it works without a connection. Logging out or clearing site data removes it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • It does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking.
  • It never sees your Google password; you sign in on Google's own page.

Finance Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting your data

  • Click Disconnect in the app to delete the stored Google sign-in token.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.
  • To delete your records, delete the "Finance Tracker" folder from your Google Drive.
